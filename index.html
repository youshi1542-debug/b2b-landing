FAQ

The questions every operator asks first

Can I switch between the three models later?

Yes, and most partners do. Pay-as-you-go is designed as a way in: if you convert to white label within 12 months, we credit half of what you have already paid in usage fees against the license.

What space and ceiling height do I need?

From 40 m² for the Lite format and 2.7 m of clear ceiling height. Columns, irregular shapes and split levels are handled in the arena map — send us a floor plan and we will tell you what fits.

Do I have to buy hardware from you?

On the franchise, the equipment package is part of the offer because support depends on a known configuration. On white label and pay-as-you-go you can source certified headsets yourself; we publish the supported list.

Which territories are available?

Franchise territories are exclusive and granted city by city. White label and pay-as-you-go have no territorial limits. Ask us about your city — we answer honestly if it is taken.

How is pay-as-you-go metered and billed?

The platform counts player-minutes inside completed sessions. Aborted or crashed sessions are not billed. You see the running total in the dashboard and receive one invoice per month.

Do you support venues outside your current countries?

We operate in 28 countries and ship to most others. Customs, certification and local language packs are handled as part of onboarding.
